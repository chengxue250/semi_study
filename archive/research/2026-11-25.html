A Solute-Aware Model Revisits Electromigration in Al-Cu Interconnects 考虑溶质演化的模型重新评估Al-Cu互连电迁移

Narahara Chari Dingari, David R. Heskett

arXiv:2611.31206 · 2026-11-23T18:53:12Z

The authors couple copper redistribution to the Korhonen stress equation on random grain maps, separating mobile dissolved copper from immobile precipitates. Simulations use 1,000 maps per condition at 200 °C for wide, narrow and near-bamboo Al-Cu lines; assuming all copper is mobile exaggerates the nucleation-time reduction by a further factor of 2.24–2.36 at 2 wt% copper. The result shows why solute state and microstructure matter in lifetime prediction, while remaining a modelling study rather than a measured device-lifetime result. 作者在随机晶粒分布上，将铜再分布与Korhonen应力方程耦合，并区分可迁移的固溶铜与不可迁移的析出相。仿真在200 °C下对宽线、窄线和近竹节状Al-Cu线的每种条件使用1000组晶粒分布；在铜含量为2 wt%时，将全部铜视为可迁移会进一步夸大成核时间的缩短，幅度达2.24至2.36倍。研究说明，寿命预测需要考虑溶质状态和微观结构，但这些仍是模型结果，而非器件寿命实测。

Lightswitch Links Digital Power-Control Requests to Physical Completion Lightswitch将数字电源控制请求与物理完成条件关联

Sabrina Yarzada, Paul Yi-Chia Chen, Yuanhan Li, et al.

arXiv:2611.30631 · 2026-11-23T12:58:57Z

This work proposes checkable logical-physical contracts for reusable on-die power-control services, combining formally verified digital guarantees with physically validated completion conditions. The Lightswitch proof of concept is assessed using post-layout designs in TSMC 28 nm. It addresses the gap between issuing a digital power request and ensuring the physical action has completed; the supplied abstract does not report fabricated-silicon measurements. 研究为可复用的片上电源控制服务提出可检查的逻辑—物理契约，将经过形式验证的数字保证与经过物理验证的完成条件结合。作者以Lightswitch为概念验证，并使用台积电28 nm工艺的布局后设计进行评估。该方法针对数字电源请求发出与物理动作实际完成之间的衔接问题；所提供的摘要未报告流片实测结果。

ChiProgramBench Tests Circuit Reconstruction Through Pin-Level Experiments ChiProgramBench通过引脚级实验测试电路重建能力

Tianrun Gou, Qingyao Yang, Wendong Xu, et al.

arXiv:2611.31082 · 2026-11-23T17:32:36Z

ChiProgramBench introduces 18 tasks in which agents probe hidden stateful circuits through their pins and submit replacement gate-level netlists. Across 432 trials with six agent configurations, only 12.0% of black-box trials and 14.8% of function-guided trials pass every hidden test. Power, delay and area receive credit only after full functional success, making the benchmark relevant to whether AI can recover both circuit behavior and efficient implementations. ChiProgramBench提出18项任务，要求智能体通过引脚探测隐藏的有状态电路，再提交替代的门级网表。在六种智能体配置的432次试验中，仅12.0%的黑盒试验和14.8%的功能提示试验通过全部隐藏测试。只有功能完全通过后，功耗、延迟和面积才计入评分，因此该基准有助于检验AI能否同时恢复电路行为并生成高效实现。

HLWalk Accelerates Thermal Analysis of Heterogeneous 2.5D and 3D ICs HLWalk加速异构2.5D与3D芯片热分析

Qibo Xue, Shengnan Cai, Tianshu Hou, et al.

arXiv:2611.30505 · 2026-11-23T11:24:29Z

HLWalk combines local boundary-integral reconstruction, modal splitting and scale-aware random walks to analyze heterogeneous 2.5D and 3D ICs. Low-order modes are solved deterministically while Monte Carlo sampling handles the residual, targeting boundary error and the cost of resolving fine structures such as TSVs. The authors report faster wall-clock execution and better accuracy on IC benchmarks, but the supplied abstract provides no numerical speedup or error bounds. HLWalk结合局部边界积分重构、模态分裂及尺度感知随机游走，对异构2.5D和3D芯片进行热分析。它确定性求解低阶模态，仅对剩余部分采用蒙特卡洛采样，以降低边界误差和解析TSV等精细结构的计算成本。作者报告其在芯片基准上运行更快、精度更高，但所提供的摘要未给出具体加速比或误差界限。

Pudding Makes Processing-Using-DRAM Compilation Retargetable Pudding支持面向DRAM内计算的可重定向编译

Tianrui Zheng, João Paulo Cardoso de Lima, Alexander Kusnezoff, et al.

arXiv:2611.30446 · 2026-11-23T10:38:33Z

Pudding proposes an MLIR-based compiler that makes a Processing-Using-DRAM architecture description an explicit input, allowing retargeting without hard-coding each architecture into the compiler. Tensor-level passes use bit-slicing, mixed precision and ILP-based placement, while logic-level equality saturation explores Boolean implementations. The aim is to reduce PUD commands and inter-bank movement across different memory-computing designs; the supplied excerpt does not include evaluation results. Pudding提出基于MLIR的编译器，将DRAM内计算架构描述作为显式输入，从而避免为每种架构在编译器中硬编码支持。张量层采用位切片、混合精度和基于整数线性规划的放置，逻辑层则通过等式饱和搜索布尔实现。目标是在不同存储计算设计间减少PUD指令和存储体间数据搬移；所提供的摘录未包含评测结果。

ANOR Splits Sparse Attention Between GPU Cache Hits and PNM Misses ANOR让GPU处理稀疏注意力缓存命中、PNM处理未命中

Hyunsu Ye, Hyunmin Choi, Jaehong Cho, et al.

arXiv:2611.30417 · 2026-11-23T10:16:06Z

ANOR proposes a GPU-PNM hierarchy that retains the full KV cache near memory while caching recently selected entries on the GPU. Most attention runs on GPU cache hits, and CXL-attached PNM devices process misses directly, allowing GPU cache updates to be separated from current-step execution. This targets the bandwidth imbalance that can leave GPUs waiting in PNM-centric systems; the supplied abstract cuts off before performance results. ANOR提出GPU与近存计算协同的层次结构，在近存侧保留完整KV-cache，并在GPU缓存近期被选中的条目。大部分注意力计算利用GPU缓存命中完成，连接CXL的PNM设备直接处理未命中，使GPU缓存更新可与当前解码步解耦。该方案针对以PNM为中心的系统中带宽不均衡导致GPU等待的问题；所提供的摘要在性能结果之前已截断。

EdgeALM Runs Large Audio Language Models on an 8 GB FPGA Card EdgeALM在8 GB FPGA卡上运行大型音频语言模型

Chenglin Yang, Feifan Xu, Xingyuan Li, et al.

arXiv:2611.30216 · 2026-11-23T07:50:16Z

EdgeALM combines AWQ-style quantization, modality-conditioned vocabulary pruning and concurrent convolution and matrix engines in an FPGA accelerator. On one Alveo U50 with 8 GB of HBM, the authors demonstrate more than 20 models, including models up to 13 billion parameters, and report audio-to-text throughput up to 169.5 tokens/s. This is a hardware implementation rather than an architecture-only proposal, although the supplied excerpt does not identify the model and operating conditions associated with peak throughput. EdgeALM在FPGA加速器中结合AWQ式量化、按模态条件进行的词表剪枝，以及并行工作的卷积与矩阵引擎。作者在一块配备8 GB HBM的Alveo U50上演示了20余个模型，其中包括最大130亿参数的模型，并报告音频转文本吞吐最高169.5 tokens/s。这提供了实际硬件实现证据，但所提供的摘录未说明峰值吞吐对应的模型及运行条件。

Inference Systems & Quantization 推理系统与量化

DECAL Calibrates Diffusion LLM Quantization for Parallel Decoding DECAL面向并行解码校准扩散大模型量化

Huan Xu, Maosen Zhao, Pengtao Chen, Tao Chen

arXiv:2611.30587 · 2026-11-23T12:27:42Z

This study finds that W4A4 quantization of diffusion LLMs causes most quantization-induced decision flips at forced token commitments, while compressed logit gaps can also increase the number of forward passes. DECAL weights calibration by commitment risk under the deployed decoder and adds an argmax-preserving commitment temperature to recover parallelism. On LLaDA and Dream it reports the best average accuracy among the compared W4A4 methods, showing why fixed-step, uncached calibration can miss deployment-specific costs. 研究发现，扩散大模型的W4A4量化所引起的决策翻转，多数出现在强制确定词元的步骤；logit间距缩小还可能增加前向传播次数。DECAL依据实际部署解码器下的提交风险调整校准权重，并使用保持argmax不变的提交温度来恢复并行度。作者报告，在LLaDA与Dream上，该方法在所比较的W4A4方案中平均准确率最高，说明固定步数且不使用缓存的校准可能遗漏部署中的特定成本。

Schism Reconfigures Resource Sharing Across Quantum Processors Schism动态重构量子处理器间的资源共享

Daniel Alexander, Deeksha P Rao, Nilesh Vyas, Vamsi Addanki

arXiv:2611.31193 · 2026-11-23T18:45:22Z

Schism proposes reconfigurable scale-up domains that change which quantum processors share Bell-state measurement resources without moving processors or migrating quantum states. Photonic switching groups processors within an optical-loss budget, balancing path loss against contention as communication patterns change. The architecture addresses uneven resource use in distributed quantum systems, but the supplied excerpt does not include quantified evaluation results. Schism提出可重构的扩展域，在不移动量子处理器或迁移量子态的情况下，改变各处理器共享Bell态测量资源的方式。光子交换在光损耗预算内对处理器分组，并随通信模式变化权衡光路损耗与资源争用。该架构针对分布式量子系统中的资源利用不均衡问题，但所提供的摘录未包含量化评测结果。
