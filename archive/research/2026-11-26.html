Circuits & Measurement 电路与测量

Calibration and integration of the GAPS silicon-tracker readout ASIC GAPS硅径迹探测器读出ASIC的校准与集成

Luca Ghislotti

arXiv:2611.32340 · 2026-11-24

This thesis reports the integration, calibration and validation of SLIDER32 readout electronics for the GAPS silicon tracker, using a custom 180 nm CMOS ASIC with lithium-drifted silicon detectors operated at −40 °C. Laboratory tests with an Am-241 source and cosmic muons were followed by full-tracker calibration in flight configuration and acquisition of a Cd-109 x-ray spectrum. The work provides instrument-level validation evidence for a demanding detector application; the supplied abstract does not quantify readout noise or energy resolution. 这篇学位论文报告了GAPS硅径迹探测器SLIDER32读出电子学的集成、校准与验证，采用定制180 nm CMOS ASIC，配合在−40 °C下工作的锂漂移硅探测器。作者先使用Am-241源和宇宙线缪子进行实验室测试，再在飞行配置下完成整套径迹探测器的校准，并获取Cd-109的X射线能谱。研究为复杂探测应用提供了仪器层面的验证证据，但所提供摘要未量化读出噪声或能量分辨率。

A 3 nm coherent DSP ASIC monitors fiber power while carrying traffic 3 nm相干DSP ASIC在传输业务时同步监测光纤功率分布

T. Sasai, Yukinobu Nakajima, Minami Takahashi, et al.

arXiv:2611.25353 · 2026-11-19

The authors implement longitudinal power monitoring in a 3 nm 800G coherent DSP ASIC inside an OSFP module, recovering spatially resolved fiber-power profiles from received traffic. Experiments over links up to 1,005 km with 400ZR+ and 800ZR+ traffic show no measurable impact on Q factor or module power consumption, and include signals from third-party transceivers. This provides hardware evidence that distributed link monitoring can coexist with data transmission under the tested conditions. 作者在OSFP模块内的3 nm、800G相干DSP ASIC上实现纵向功率监测，从接收业务信号中恢复沿光纤分布的功率曲线。在最长1005 km链路上，使用400ZR+和800ZR+业务开展的实验未测得Q因子或模块功耗受到影响，并验证了来自第三方收发器的信号。结果以实际硬件证明，在所测试条件下，分布式链路监测能够与数据传输同时运行。

AI & Domain-Specific Accelerators AI与领域专用加速器

Differential time representation supports modular SNN acceleration 差分时间表示支持模块化脉冲神经网络加速

D. Windhager, L. Ratschbacher, B. Moser, et al.

arXiv:2611.32758 · 2026-11-24

This work represents spike trains with Differential Time Representation, using fewer bits than address-event encoding or uniformly sampled one-bit trains, and proves that multiple streams can be merged with low complexity. Modular streaming hardware and a Python framework generate parallel and time-multiplexed accelerators for SHD, SSC and PSMNIST. The authors report FPGA and ASIC implementations, but the supplied abstract ends before quantitative energy and area results, limiting comparisons with other SNN accelerators. 研究采用差分时间表示编码脉冲序列，相比地址事件表示或等间隔采样的单比特序列使用更少位数，并证明多个序列可以低复杂度合并。模块化流式硬件与Python框架可为SHD、SSC和PSMNIST生成并行版及分时复用版加速器。作者报告了FPGA与ASIC实现，但所提供摘要在能耗和面积的量化结果之前截断，因此暂不足以与其他SNN加速器作定量比较。

VEGA extends RISC-V vectors for programmable genome alignment VEGA扩展RISC-V向量指令以加速可编程基因序列比对

Wei-Hong Xu, Shi-Jie Xu, Qun-You Liu, et al.

arXiv:2611.28741 · 2026-11-21

VEGA combines RISC-V vector instruction extensions, a Forefront Stationary dataflow and intersequence parallelism to accelerate genome alignment while reducing off-chip accesses and improving SIMD utilization. Against Ara2-RVV, it reports peak speedups of 15.1× for Edit Distance and 9.7× for Smith–Waterman, retaining 10.2× and 6.7× gains for 10 kbp sequences. Post-synthesis results report 1.0% area overhead relative to a standard RISC-V core; these are synthesis and evaluation results rather than measured fabricated-silicon performance. VEGA结合RISC-V向量指令扩展、Forefront Stationary数据流及序列间并行，在加速基因序列比对的同时减少片外访问、提高SIMD利用率。相对Ara2-RVV，Edit Distance与Smith–Waterman的峰值加速比分别为15.1倍和9.7倍，在10 kbp序列上仍分别达到10.2倍和6.7倍。综合后结果显示，相对标准RISC-V核的面积开销为1.0%；这些属于综合与评估结果，并非流片芯片的性能实测。

A rover integrates neuromorphic audio and event-based vision 移动机器人集成神经形态音频与事件视觉

Guillem Fustier-Castillo, Ruben Ribes-Serrano, Javier Naranjo-Alcazar, et al.

arXiv:2611.27953 · 2026-11-20

This technical report integrates three real-time perception pipelines on a four-wheel rover: PANNs audio tagging on an NVIDIA Jetson, SNN audio processing on a SynSense Xylo Audio 2 chip, and SpikeYOLO detection of traffic cones from event-camera input. The Xylo audio path performs microphone-to-classification processing on-chip, while containerized services connect through ROS 2. The contribution is system integration on a robotic platform; the supplied abstract gives no quantitative end-to-end latency or energy comparison. 这份技术报告在四轮移动机器人上集成了三条实时感知流程：NVIDIA Jetson上的PANNs音频标注、SynSense Xylo Audio 2芯片上的SNN音频处理，以及利用事件相机输入的SpikeYOLO交通锥检测。Xylo音频通路从麦克风信号到分类均在片上完成，各容器化服务通过ROS 2连接。研究贡献在于机器人平台上的系统集成；所提供摘要未给出端到端时延或能耗的定量对比。

Design Automation & Validation 设计自动化与验证

Learning passive interconnect and power-delivery macromodels directly from layouts 从版图直接学习无源互连与供电网络宏模型

Ben-Chao Zhu, Ke-Ren Zhu

arXiv:2611.28778 · 2026-11-21

The authors map physical layout graphs directly to multi-order port-Hamiltonian macromodels whose parameterization guarantees continuous-frequency passivity and stability by construction. One forward pass yields models of different orders through state slicing, with training supervised by S-parameters rather than reference state-space realizations. Evaluation covers 27,000 unseen structures from a 270,000-structure advanced-packaging dataset, but the supplied abstract truncates before the numerical error results. The method targets stable signal- and power-integrity simulation without a separate passivity-repair step. 作者将物理版图图结构直接映射为多阶端口哈密顿宏模型，通过参数化结构本身保证连续频域的无源性与稳定性。模型一次前向传播即可通过状态切片获得不同阶数，训练直接以S参数为监督，无需参考状态空间实现。评估使用包含27万个结构的先进封装数据集中的2.7万个未见结构，但所提供摘要在具体误差结果之前截断。该方法面向稳定的信号完整性与电源完整性仿真，无需额外的无源性修复步骤。

A survey maps validation methods for heterogeneous hardware-software co-design 综述梳理异构软硬件协同设计的验证方法

Oceane Bel, Andrés Márquez, Dhruv Gajaria, et al.

ACM Transactions on Modeling and Computer Simulation · 2026-11-19

This survey reviews co-simulation and benchmarking for systems combining chiplets, 2.5D interposers, 3D stacks, RF modules and photonics. It connects SystemC and TLM models, gem5 full-system simulation and FPGA emulation with packaging validation, focusing on scalability, synchronization and multiphysics limitations. The paper offers a map of validation approaches for performance, energy, thermal behavior and reliability, rather than a new accelerator or a measured speedup. 这篇综述讨论了包含Chiplet、2.5D中介层、3D堆叠、RF模块和光子器件的系统所需的协同仿真与基准测试。文章将SystemC与TLM模型、gem5全系统仿真和FPGA仿真验证，与封装验证流程联系起来，重点分析可扩展性、同步及多物理场建模的限制。其价值在于梳理性能、能耗、热行为和可靠性的验证方法，而非提出新的加速器或报告实测加速比。
