NORmal: Bringing 3D Ferroelectric NOR Flash Onto an LPDDR6 Memory Interface NORmal：将3D铁电NOR闪存接入LPDDR6内存接口

Zhuoran Li, Zhuohang Bian, Yibo Zhao, et al.

arXiv:2610.40000 · 2026-10-29T13:19:45Z

NORmal reports fabricated BEOL 3D ferroelectric NOR arrays combining dense 3-bit MLC storage with fast, writable SLC and sub-7.5 ns sensing. It proposes a DRAM-emulation bridge that presents these arrays as a byte-addressable rank on an unmodified LPDDR6 interface. The approach aims to expand local AI memory capacity while accommodating mutable runtime data. The sensing measurement leaves controller, interface, and complete-system timing as separate evaluation questions. NORmal报告了已制备的后道互连层（BEOL）3D铁电NOR阵列，将高密度3-bit MLC存储与快速、可写的SLC集成，感测时间低于7.5 ns。研究提出通过DRAM仿真桥，将这些阵列作为可按字节寻址的rank接入未经修改的LPDDR6接口。该方案旨在扩大本地AI的内存容量，同时容纳运行时更新的数据。感测时间这一实测指标之外，控制器、接口与完整系统的时序仍需分别评估。

A 65nm CiROM Macro Stores Two Ternary Weights per Cell for 1.58-bit LLM Inference 面向1.58-bit LLM推理的65nm CiROM宏单元：每个单元存储两个三值权重

Wen-Lun Zhang, Xin-Yu Li, Shimpei Ando, et al.

IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I: Regular Papers · 2026-11-01

A 65nm compute-in-ROM macro uses configurable source and bit lines to store two ternary weights in each 1T ROM cell for BitNet-style inference. A local accumulator gates zero-weight operations, while a digital adapter co-designed with LoRA provides downstream task adaptation after fabrication. The design links storage density and sparsity to circuit-level switching reductions, with fixed ROM weights constraining how broadly a deployed chip can be retargeted. 这项65nm ROM内计算宏单元设计通过可配置源极线与位线，在每个1T ROM单元中存储两个三值权重，面向BitNet类推理。本地累加器对零权重运算实施门控，与LoRA协同设计的数字适配器则支持流片后的下游任务适配。设计将存储密度和稀疏性利用落实到电路开关活动的削减，但固定的ROM权重也会约束芯片部署后可迁移的任务范围。

Workload-Static Optical Circuit Switching for Scalable LLM Decode 以工作负载静态光路交换扩展LLM解码互连

Maximilian Thürmer, Nicolas Scotto di Perto, Eloise Stein, et al.

arXiv:2611.05104 · 2026-11-03T16:00:07Z

This optical circuit-switching design configures links once per model deployment and co-designs collective schedules with model parallelism. For a modeled 576-accelerator domain serving 5T-parameter MoE models with contexts up to 5M tokens, it reports up to 65% better interactivity and 119% better energy efficiency than a 4:1 oversubscribed packet-switched fabric. The gains come from GXNET workload emulation and analytical and flit-level network models grounded in hardware measurements, making oversubscription and communication schedules key evaluation assumptions. 这项光路交换设计在每次模型部署时配置一次链路，并将集合通信调度与模型并行方式协同优化。在模拟的576颗加速器域中，运行5万亿参数MoE模型、上下文最长500万token时，相较4:1超额订阅的分组交换网络，交互性能最高提升65%，能效最高提升119%。这些结果来自GXNET工作负载仿真及以硬件测量为依据的分析模型、flit级网络模型，超额订阅比例与通信调度是理解收益的关键评估条件。

TinyML Benchmarks Ten Sub-Watt Devices and Exposes Memory Constraints TinyML对比十款亚瓦级器件，检视存储约束

Pietro Bonazzi, Luigi Capogrosso, Ahmet Celik, et al.

arXiv:2611.03392 · 2026-11-02T16:28:53Z

A benchmark evaluates four CNNs and two small transformers across ten commercial edge devices, including sensor-integrated processing and MCUs with and without NPUs. For the reported energy-above-idle measurements, Sony's IMX500 uses 0.13–0.35 mJ versus 0.31–1.05 mJ for STM32N6; different models also favor GAP9 or Alif E7. The workload-dependent rankings show why platform selection needs measured latency and energy alongside operation counts, with the quoted energy figures excluding idle consumption. 这项基准在十款商用边缘器件上评估了四种CNN与两种小型Transformer，覆盖传感器内处理器以及有、无NPU的MCU。按高于空闲基线的增量能耗计算，Sony IMX500为0.13–0.35 mJ，STM32N6为0.31–1.05 mJ；部分模型则以GAP9或Alif E7表现更优。排名随工作负载变化，说明平台选型需要结合实测时延与能耗评估运算量，上述能耗数值未计入空闲消耗。

Streaming NTT Across Three FPGA Dies for Homomorphic Encryption 跨三个FPGA芯粒的流式NTT加速同态加密

Mohamed Allam, Thomas Spendlhofer, Hugo Sanz-González, Antonio J. Peña

arXiv:2611.03325 · 2026-11-02T15:49:14Z

An NTT/INTT kernel mapped across the AMD Alveo V80's three dies reports processing a 65,536-coefficient polynomial every 5.12 microseconds at 400 MHz. Its fully unrolled delay-commutator streaming structure addresses the routing overhead that can grow with parallelism on multi-die FPGAs. This targets a major cost in homomorphic-encryption inference, with application speedup also depending on transfers and the remaining FHE pipeline. 这项NTT/INTT内核映射到AMD Alveo V80的三个芯粒，在400 MHz下每5.12微秒可处理一个含65,536个系数的多项式。完全展开的延迟交换器流式结构针对多芯粒FPGA中随并行度上升的布线开销。该设计瞄准同态加密推理的一项主要计算成本，应用整体加速比还取决于数据传输及FHE流水线的其他部分。

UMLoad Accelerates On-Demand Model Loading on Unified Memory UMLoad加速统一内存平台的按需模型加载

Zimeng Wang, Jinrong Yang, Rong Chen

arXiv:2611.04253 · 2026-11-03T03:44:38Z

UMLoad reduces average on-demand model loading time by 36.6–71.9% against unmodified loaders and 19.9–62.7% against pipelined loaders in tests across Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA unified-memory platforms. It separates logical allocations from physical page backing, eliminating weight copies through on-demand reconstruction and accelerating allocation with GPU-assisted page zeroing. Mean time to first token in LLM-routing tests falls 51.3–59.3%, making loader design relevant to interactive latency; the evaluation covers six models and the tested software stacks. 在Intel、AMD与NVIDIA统一内存平台的测试中，UMLoad相较原始加载器将平均按需模型加载时间缩短36.6–71.9%，相较流水化加载器缩短19.9–62.7%。它将逻辑分配与物理页后备解耦，通过按需重建消除权重复制，并借助GPU辅助页清零加速内存分配。LLM路由测试的平均首token时延降低51.3–59.3%，显示加载器设计会显著影响交互响应；评估范围为六个模型及所测试的软件栈。

PagedMoE Reuses Expert Cache Data Across Precision Levels PagedMoE跨精度复用专家缓存数据

Yutong Lin, Yingdi Shan, Shaoyuan Chen, et al.

arXiv:2611.03173 · 2026-11-02T14:09:55Z

PagedMoE stores each SSD-resident MoE expert in a prefix-compatible representation, allowing cached low-precision data to be extended by fetching only missing bitplanes. It selects precision before I/O using routing weights and expert-specific error profiles, aiming to avoid repeated transfers when requested bit widths change. The design targets DRAM-limited local inference, with practical throughput and quality depending on cache capacity, SSD behavior, and the precision-selection policy. PagedMoE将驻留SSD的每个MoE专家存成前缀兼容的表示，使已缓存的低精度数据能够通过补取缺失位平面扩展到更高精度。系统利用路由权重与各专家的误差特征，在I/O发生前选择精度，力求避免所需位宽变化时重复搬运相同数据。该设计面向DRAM容量受限的本地推理，实际吞吐量与模型质量取决于缓存容量、SSD特性及精度选择策略。

APPlace Co-Optimizes Analog Device Placement and Pin Access Points APPlace联合优化模拟器件布局与引脚接入点

Bingyang Liu, Xiaohan Gao, Souradip Poddar, David Z. Pan

arXiv:2610.39515 · 2026-10-29T07:14:08Z

APPlace jointly optimizes device coordinates and routing access-point selections during analog global placement, then preserves that structure through legalization. Across four real-world analog benchmarks, it reports better overall post-layout performance than representative baselines at comparable layout area. Modeling the actual connection choices gives placement a more useful view of downstream routing, with the reported evidence scoped to those benchmark circuits. APPlace在模拟电路全局布局阶段联合优化器件坐标与布线接入点选择，并在合法化阶段保留已建立的结构。在四个实际模拟电路基准上，研究报告其在布局面积相近时，整体版图后性能优于代表性基线方法。将实际连接位置纳入建模，有助于布局阶段更准确地考虑后续布线，现有结果的验证范围为这些基准电路。
