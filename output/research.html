Training-free focus maps cut encoder FLOPs in long-video segmentation 免训练聚焦图降低长视频分割的编码器FLOPs

Harsha Karipineni, Rajesh Reddy Chintha

arXiv:2611.34863 · 2026-11-25T18:30:28Z

A training-free focus map preserves detailed tokens around tracked objects while merging background tokens in a frozen DINOv3 encoder inside a trained Cutie segmenter. On LVOS V2 with ViT-S, the authors report a 2.76-point J&F gain over ALGM 0.98 with 16% fewer counted encoder FLOPs, while remaining 1.0 point below the dense encoder. Motion-aware masks and a camera-compensated surprise check aim to prevent targets from being merged into the background. These are benchmark and operation-count results, not measured reductions in hardware latency or energy. 该方法在已训练的Cutie分割器中，为冻结的DINOv3编码器加入免训练聚焦图，保留跟踪目标周围的细粒度token，并合并背景token。作者报告，在LVOS V2的ViT-S实验中，相较ALGM 0.98，J&F提高2.76个百分点，统计的编码器FLOPs减少16%，但仍比稠密编码器低1.0个百分点。运动感知掩码和相机运动补偿后的异常检查用于避免目标被误并入背景。这些属于基准测试与运算量统计结果，不能直接等同于硬件时延或能耗降幅。

arXiv:2611.34726 · 2026-11-25T17:12:08Z

Hierarchical Graph Metanetworks add pooling and unpooling that preserve permutation symmetry while moving between neuron-, layer- and block-level representations of a neural network. Magnetometry detects internal battery leakage down to about 1–3 mA 磁测量可检测约1至3 mA的电池内部漏电流

Shaurya Shukla, Chengtong Zhang, Yinan Hu, et al.

arXiv:2611.34699 · 2026-11-25T16:54:34Z

A fixed three-axis magnetoresistive sensor and a scanning stage map pouch-cell magnetic fields, detecting internal leakage of about 1–3 mA at rest. Two mechanically indented 600 mAh cells developed a decaying magnetic field at zero applied current, while an intact cell showed a linear field response from 10 to 420 mA. The measured asymmetry between charging and discharging offers a diagnostic signal that terminal measurements can miss. The setup uses three-layer magnetic shielding and a small damaged-cell sample, leaving production-line sensitivity and throughput unresolved. 研究以固定的三轴磁阻传感器和扫描位移平台绘制软包电池磁场分布，在静置状态下可检测约1至3 mA的内部漏电流。2块受到机械压痕损伤的600 mAh电池在外加电流为零时产生了随时间衰减的磁场，完好电池则在10至420 mA范围内呈线性磁场响应。充放电磁场的不对称性提供了端口测量可能遗漏的诊断信号。实验使用三层磁屏蔽，受损样本数量也较少，产线条件下的灵敏度和检测吞吐量仍待验证。
