SemiWiki · 7h ago SemiWiki · 7小时前

A SemiWiki analysis argues that faster HBM alone cannot determine the performance of next-generation AI accelerators. HBM4 widens the interface and increases available bandwidth, while custom HBM opens opportunities to optimize the memory-to-accelerator connection. The implication for designers is to assess memory as part of the complete system rather than use peak bandwidth as a proxy for application performance. SemiWiki的一篇分析指出，仅提高HBM速度，无法决定下一代AI加速器的实际性能。HBM4通过加宽接口提高可用带宽，定制HBM则为优化存储与加速器之间的连接提供了空间。对设计者而言，需要把存储放在完整系统中评估，不能直接用峰值带宽代替应用性能。
