Researchers at RPI and IBM propose REACH, a controller-managed ECC approach intended to reduce HBM error-correction overhead during AI inference. The work matters because HBM efficiency, not nominal bandwidth alone, increasingly limits accelerator utilization. RPI与IBM研究人员提出REACH，这是一种由控制器管理的ECC方案，目标是降低AI推理期间HBM纠错开销。把更多可靠性管理放到控制器侧，有望释放原本被保护逻辑占用的带宽与容量。随着HBM效率而非名义带宽日益限制加速器利用率，这项工作具有直接的系统意义。
