Wccftech · 9h ago Wccftech · 9小时前

AMD says a hybrid-bonded 3D DRAM design is 17 times more energy-efficient than an HBM stack connected with microbumps. Removing bump-scale interconnect overhead could materially reduce the energy cost of feeding AI compute, but AMD also identifies thermal constraints as a barrier to commercialization. The result is therefore a roadmap signal, not evidence of a production-ready HBM replacement. AMD表示，采用混合键合的3D DRAM设计，其能效可达到微凸点互连HBM堆栈的17倍。消除微凸点尺度的互连开销，有望显著降低为AI计算供数的能耗，但AMD同时指出散热约束仍阻碍商业化。因此，这更像是路线图信号，而不是已经可量产的HBM替代方案。
