U.S. Targets Remote Access to Advanced AI Chips 美国将先进AI芯片的远程访问纳入管制

Tom's Hardware · 6h ago Tom's Hardware · 6小时前

The U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule targeting access to advanced AI chips and servers via data centers outside China and other arms-embargoed markets. The measure adds licensing requirements for certain advanced computing shipments and transfers involving Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. Chip vendors and cloud operators now have to consider who will use the compute alongside where the hardware will be installed. 美国商务部工业与安全局发布临时最终规则，针对中国等受武器禁运国家和地区通过境外数据中心远程使用先进AI芯片和服务器的情形。规则对涉及巴西、印度尼西亚、马来西亚、新加坡和泰国的部分先进计算产品出口、再出口和境内转移新增许可要求。芯片供应商和云服务商在评估合规时，需要同时考虑设备部署地点和算力的实际使用者。

Nvidia Unveils Rubin HPC GPU for 2028 英伟达发布Rubin HPC GPU，预计2028年供货

Tom's Hardware · 9h ago Tom's Hardware · 9小时前

Nvidia unveiled Rubin HPC at SC26 and expects the scientific-computing GPU to be available in 2028. The company specifies more than 50,000 CUDA cores per GPU, acceleration for sparse data operations and seventh-generation NVLink. The design targets workloads such as simulations, where getting irregularly accessed data to the cores can limit performance. 英伟达在SC26发布面向科学计算的Rubin HPC GPU，预计于2028年供货。据公司披露，单颗GPU配有逾5万个CUDA核心，支持稀疏数据操作加速和第七代NVLink互连。该设计面向仿真等工作负载，这类任务往往会因数据访问不规则、计算核心等待数据而受限。

Digital Today · 4h ago Digital Today · 4小时前

SemiFive unveiled an SC26 packaging platform that stacks DRAM directly above logic and uses hundreds of thousands of connections between the dies. The company says shorter signal paths can lower energy per bit, while eliminating the interposer could reduce packaging costs. The platform gives custom AI chip designers a way to pursue high memory bandwidth with a different package layout from standard HBM. SemiFive在SC26发布一款3D封装平台，将DRAM直接堆叠在逻辑芯片上方，两者之间设置数十万条物理互连通道。公司称，缩短信号传输距离有望降低每比特能耗，省去中介层则有望降低封装成本。这为定制AI芯片提供了一条实现高内存带宽的路径。

CASRAI · 6h ago CASRAI · 6小时前

Intel and SambaNova demonstrated a disaggregated inference system at SC26 that uses Intel D270 GPUs for prompt prefill and SambaNova SN50 reconfigurable dataflow units for token generation. Both systems use air cooling.
