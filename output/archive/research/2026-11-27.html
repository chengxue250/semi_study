DFly2 shares a sparse target's KV cache and position selection DFly2共享稀疏目标模型的KV缓存与位置选择结果

Hong Liu, Rui Cen, Junhan Shi, et al.

arXiv:2611.34776 · 2026-11-25T17:37:49Z

DFly2 uses a block-parallel draft model that shares a sparse target model's KV cache and attention-position selection, avoiding a separate draft cache and redundant selection work. The authors report that a selection made one step earlier still covers close to 90% of positions selected by later tokens on DS-V4.1-Flash. This targets memory traffic, prefill overhead and serving concurrency in long-context speculative decoding. The supplied abstract is truncated before end-to-end performance results, so the overlap figure should not be read as a measured speedup. DFly2采用块并行草稿模型，共享稀疏目标模型的KV缓存与注意力位置选择结果，从而避免独立草稿缓存及重复的位置筛选。作者报告，在DS-V4.1-Flash上，提前一步生成的选择结果仍能覆盖后续token所选位置的近90%。该方法针对长上下文推测解码的内存流量、预填充开销和服务并发约束。所提供摘要在端到端性能结果之前截断，因此这一覆盖率不能解读为实测加速比。

Mixture-of-Loops Transformer reuses KV memory across depth and loops Mixture-of-Loops Transformer跨深度与循环复用KV记忆

Lianghui Zhu, Shijie Wang, Xinggang Wang, et al.

arXiv:2611.34923 · 2026-11-25T18:58:53Z

Mixture-of-Loops Transformer changes what passes between repeated Transformer loops by using keys and values as selectively reusable memory. Its U-shaped variant separates memory construction from prediction readout and skips unused decoder traversals without changing predictions. The authors report outperforming OLMo3 after 400B-token pretraining across 700M- and 1.5B-parameter dense models and a 3B MoE model. The design changes cache access and execution scheduling, but the supplied abstract ends before numerical efficiency gains or hardware latency measurements. Mixture-of-Loops Transformer以key和value作为可选择复用的记忆，改变重复Transformer循环之间传递的信息。其U形变体将记忆构建与预测读出分离，并在不改变预测的情况下跳过无用的解码器遍历。作者报告，经过4000亿token预训练后，7亿和15亿参数的稠密模型及30亿参数MoE模型均优于OLMo3。该设计改变了缓存访问与执行调度，但所提供摘要在具体效率收益或硬件时延测量之前截断。

LoopTitan separates parameter reuse from other looped-MoE gains LoopTitan区分参数复用与循环MoE的其他收益来源

Zichen Tian, Yudong Lu, Xuansheng Wu, et al.

arXiv:2611.34821 · 2026-11-25T18:04:31Z

LoopTitan finds that parameter revisiting itself contributes almost no gain in its matched-budget MoE experiments once network shape and residual scaling are controlled. A model without revisiting retains the reported improvement, with most attributed to network shape; the residual-scaling benefit disappears under Muon in the authors' analysis. The result argues for evaluating depth-versus-expert allocation before adding recurrent execution to an inference system. It concerns the tested recipes and controls rather than establishing that all forms of parameter reuse are ineffective. LoopTitan在参数量、FLOPs与KV缓存预算匹配的MoE实验中发现，控制网络形状和残差缩放后，重复访问参数本身几乎不带来收益。无需重复访问的对照模型保留了所报告的提升，其中大部分来自网络形状；作者分析中的残差缩放收益则在采用Muon后消失。这提示推理系统在引入循环执行前，应先评估深度与专家数量的分配。结论针对所测试的训练方案与对照条件，并不等于证明所有参数复用方式都无效。

Chorus N-gram tables expose a memory-versus-backbone tradeoff Chorus N-gram查找表揭示存储与主干规模的权衡

Szymon Kocur

arXiv:2611.34725 · 2026-11-25T17:10:46Z

Experiments with 16M–349M-parameter models find that Chorus bigram and trigram hash tables can match a larger plain Transformer's held-out loss, but replace relatively little backbone capacity per added parameter. With tables roughly as large as the backbone, 107M–349M models match plain models 1.16–1.20× their size, while each added parameter replaces at most 0.31 backbone parameters. Chorus trains slower than a same-size plain model and faster than one with the same loss. The memory-compute tradeoff is measured on one historical Polish corpus, one 8,000-token vocabulary and one-fifth of an epoch. 对1600万至3.49亿参数模型的实验发现，Chorus二元与三元token哈希表可使留出集损失达到更大普通Transformer的水平，但每个新增参数替代的主干容量有限。当查找表规模约等于主干时，1.07亿至3.49亿参数模型可匹配规模为其1.16至1.20倍的普通模型，而每个新增参数最多替代0.31个主干参数。Chorus的训练速度低于同规模普通模型，但高于损失相同的普通模型。这一存储与计算权衡仅在单一历史波兰语语料、8000 token词表及五分之一个训练轮次上评估。

LLift prepares one checkpoint for multiple SVD compression budgets LLift使单个检查点适配多种SVD压缩预算

Chenyue Fang, Danila Mishin, Babak Falsafi

arXiv:2611.34667 · 2026-11-25T16:35:40Z

LLift fine-tunes a low-rank approximation at one compression ratio using the original model as a teacher, then merges the updates while preserving weight components excluded from that forward pass. The prepared checkpoint reuses SVD decompositions at different truncation ranks without separately fine-tuning every compressed model. On LLaMA-7B, the authors report accuracy gains at three compression ratios and extensions across model families, sizes and SVD operators. This could simplify deployment across memory budgets, although the abstract gives no quantified accuracy gains or inference-speed measurements. LLift以原始模型为教师，在单一压缩比例下微调低秩近似，再合并更新，同时保留训练前向传播中未使用的权重分量。处理后的检查点可在不同截断秩下复用SVD分解，无需为每个压缩模型分别微调。作者在LLaMA-7B的3种压缩比例下报告精度改善，并将结果扩展至不同模型家族、规模和SVD压缩算子。这可能简化面向不同内存预算的部署，但摘要未量化精度增益或推理速度。

Selective transfer learning adapts autoencoder communication front ends 选择性迁移学习适配自编码器通信前端

Yalin E. Sagduyu, Tugba Erpek

arXiv:2611.34942 · 2026-11-25T18:59:57Z

This study adapts an autoencoder-based physical layer to channel mismatch by fine-tuning a small subset of transmitter and receiver layers while freezing the rest. It formulates layer selection as a budget-constrained optimization problem and reports removing a mismatch-induced error floor when moving from AWGN training to Rayleigh fading with front-end impairments. The approach targets the time and energy cost of updating learned communications systems. The supplied abstract is truncated and provides no measured silicon power, adaptation latency or complete BER comparison. 这项研究通过仅微调发射端与接收端的少数层、冻结其余层，使基于自编码器的物理层适应信道失配。作者将层选择建模为预算约束下的优化问题，并报告在从AWGN训练条件转向带前端非理想因素的瑞利衰落信道时，消除了失配引起的误码率平台。该方法针对学习式通信系统更新时的时间与能耗开销。所提供摘要存在截断，未给出芯片功耗实测、适配时延或完整的误码率对比。
